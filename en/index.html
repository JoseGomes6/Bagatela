Frequently asked questionsBefore you ask…

Can't find the answer? Talk to us, we reply quickly.

How long does it take to get the site ready?

In most cases the site goes live a few days after we receive your texts and photos.

Are the domain and hosting included?

We take care of registering the .pt domain and the hosting. Annual costs are shown clearly in the proposal.

Can I request changes later?

Yes. You can request one-off changes at 25€/hour or sign up for the optional monthly maintenance at 50€/month, which includes 2 hours of changes per month, plus security updates, backups, site monitoring and a monthly report.

Will my site show up on Google?

All sites follow SEO best practice: fast loading, mobile version, optimised titles and descriptions and Google Search Console registration.

I don't have texts or photos. Now what?

We help write the texts and show you how to take good photos with your phone. We can also use free stock images or, if you prefer, arrange a photographer for an additional cost.
